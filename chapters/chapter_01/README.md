# Chapter 1: The Agent-Augmented Analytics Playbook

## Purpose

Examine one recurring analytical workflow and identify a credible opportunity for agent augmentation. The companion skill helps you produce an opportunity brief for review.

## Before you start

Choose a workflow you know. Bring a recent report, notebook, process description, or other evidence of how the work currently happens. You can also examine a proposed workflow, keeping its assumptions explicit.

## Materials

Use the [Brainstorm an Agent-Augmented Workflow](../../skills/brainstorm-agent-augmented-workflow/SKILL.md) skill to explore an opportunity using your own organization's work and context.

## Work through the chapter

1. Attach or paste the skill's instructions into your agent conversation, or use your tool's supported skill-loading mechanism. Cloning this repository alone does not make the skill automatically available to your tool.
2. Introduce your workflow and supply relevant materials. Use the prompt below to start the discussion.
3. Review the emerging opportunity, correct assumptions, and decide what to carry forward.

Suggested opening prompt:

> Use the brainstorm-agent-augmented-workflow skill to help me assess one recurring analytical workflow. Ask one primary question at a time. Inspect the materials I provide, distinguish facts from hypotheses, and help me select an opportunity before we design the workflow.

## What you should have

A reviewed opportunity brief describing the workflow, the decision it supports, possible agent contributions, unresolved risks, retained human accountability, and an agreed next action. The discussion may also establish that the candidate is not worth pursuing.

The brief is a starting point for later workflow design. It does not authorize execution of an analysis or constitute a completed playbook.
